## Block websites

1. Go to **Controls**, then **Websites and internet**.
2. Tap **Allow** and accept Android's VPN request. A key icon appears in the status bar while it is on.
3. Tap the sites you want to block under **Popular**, or type your own under **Add your own** (a full link is fine).
4. Choose **Always**, or **During focus & schedules**.

Blocked sites show "This site can't be reached" in every browser. Blocking a site also stops its app on your phone, because apps use the same addresses.

## Internet off

Switch on **Internet off during focus & schedules** to take every app offline while you focus, except calls, messages, alarms and your always-allowed apps.

## How it works

STOIC's VPN never leaves your phone. It only answers "where is this website?" questions, saying "nowhere" for the sites you block and passing every other question to your usual network. It can't see what you read or type, and nothing is sent to STOIC.

<IfItDidntWork>

- **A blocked site still opens**: browsers remember addresses for a minute or two. Wait, or close and reopen the tab.
- **Nothing is blocked**: if you set a custom **Private DNS** in Android's settings, website blocking can't work. Set it to **Automatic**.
- **The key icon is gone**: only one VPN can run at a time. Turning on another VPN app switches STOIC's off. Tap **Start again**.

</IfItDidntWork>
