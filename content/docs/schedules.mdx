## Bedtime

1. Go to **Controls**, then **Schedules and bedtime**.
2. Switch on **Bedtime**.
3. Tap **Starts** and **Ends** to pick the times. A bedtime that ends in the morning is fine: it runs past midnight.
4. Pick the nights it starts on.
5. Choose **All apps** or **Only apps I pick**.
6. Optional: switch on **Turn on Do Not Disturb**. Android asks you to allow STOIC under **Do Not Disturb access** the first time.

During Bedtime, opening a blocked app shows "It's bedtime" and when it ends.

## Your own schedules

Tap **Add a schedule** to make one, for example **Study hours** on weekdays from 9 to 5. Give it a name, times, days and the apps it blocks. You can make as many as you like and pause any of them.

## What always works

Your phone app, messages, alarm clock, home screen and Settings are never blocked by a schedule, so you can always be reached and woken up. You can add more under **Always allowed** (these are skipped by schedules set to **All apps**).

## Do Not Disturb

STOIC switches on "priority only" Do Not Disturb when the schedule starts and off when it ends. Alarms and the people you allow in Android's Do Not Disturb settings still get through. If you changed Do Not Disturb yourself in the meantime, STOIC leaves it alone.

<Note>

**Let me add 5 more minutes** offers a short snooze on the bedtime screen. Switch it off to make the schedule strict until it ends.

</Note>

<IfItDidntWork>

- **Apps are not blocked**: check **STOIC limits and pauses** under **Accessibility**.
- **Do Not Disturb did not come on**: check STOIC under **Do Not Disturb access**, and that Do Not Disturb was off when the schedule started.

</IfItDidntWork>
