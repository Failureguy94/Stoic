## Start a session

1. Go to the **Focus** tab.
2. Pick **15**, **25** or **45** minutes.
3. Tap **Start**, put the phone down, and watch Pebble grow.

Ending early is fine: the minutes you did still count towards your week and your day score.

## Make it stronger

- **Tell me when it ends** sends one notification from your phone when time is up.
- **Block my chosen apps while I focus** blocks the apps you marked **Block during focus sessions** under **Limits and pauses**.
- Under **Websites and internet** you can block sites **During focus & schedules**, or switch on **Internet off during focus & schedules**.
- During **Strict mode**, a session that blocks apps can't be ended early.

<Note>

Focus sessions are saved only on this phone. There is no streak to lose: every session just adds up.

</Note>
