## Pauses, limits or schedules stopped working

1. Open Android **Settings**, then **Accessibility**, and check that **STOIC limits and pauses** is on. Android switches it off when STOIC is force-stopped, and after STOIC is updated from a file.
2. Let STOIC run in the background. On many phones, battery saving stops apps like STOIC:
   - **Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco**: Settings, Apps, STOIC: turn on **Autostart** and set **Battery saver** to **No restrictions**.
   - **Samsung**: Settings, Battery, Background usage limits: add STOIC to **Never sleeping apps**.
   - **Oppo, Realme, OnePlus**: Settings, Apps, STOIC, Battery: allow **background activity**.
3. Open STOIC once. That also restarts its background work.

## Android says "Restricted setting"

Open **Settings**, **Apps**, **STOIC**, tap the three dots at the top right and choose **Allow restricted settings**. Then switch the permission on again.

## Websites are not blocked

- Check for the key icon in the status bar. If it is missing, open **Websites and internet** and tap **Start again**.
- Set **Private DNS** (in Android's network settings) to **Automatic**.

## My screen time shows nothing

Switch on **Usage access** for STOIC. If your phone's clock was changed by a lot, Android itself may lose the day's usage; it fixes itself from the next day.
