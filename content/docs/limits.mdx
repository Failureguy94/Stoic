## Add an app

1. Go to **Controls**, then **Limits and pauses**.
2. Tap **Add an app** and pick it from the list (you can search).
3. Choose any mix of the options below. Every change is saved straight away.

## The options

- **Pause before opening**: a 10 second pause asking "Is this important right now?". **Open anyway** appears after the countdown. Once you open anyway, STOIC leaves you alone for that visit (and three minutes after it).
- **Daily limit** (15 minutes to 2 hours): once you reach it, you see "That's your time for today". It starts fresh at midnight. Needs **Usage access**.
- **Opens per day** (5 to 40): after that many opens, the app shows "That's your opens for today". An open counts only once you actually get into the app, and coming back within 30 seconds (for example from a share sheet) is the same visit.
- **Check in after** (10, 20 or 30 minutes in a row): STOIC asks "Still worth it?". **Keep going** restarts the count. This is about time in the app; STOIC does not watch what you scroll.
- **Let me go a little over**: offers **5 more minutes** on a daily limit and **Open once more** on the opens limit. Switch it off to make both strict until midnight.
- **Block during focus sessions**: blocks the app while a Pebble focus session runs, if you also switch on **Block my chosen apps while I focus** on the Focus tab.

## When several rules apply

The strictest one shows: a focus session first, then a schedule (like Bedtime), then the daily limit, then opens per day, then the check-in, then the pause.

<IfItDidntWork>

- **Nothing happens when I open the app**: check that **STOIC limits and pauses** is on under **Accessibility**. Android switches it off after STOIC is updated from a file, or if you force-stop STOIC.
- **The daily limit never shows**: switch on **Usage access** for STOIC.

</IfItDidntWork>
