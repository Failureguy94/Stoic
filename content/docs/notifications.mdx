## See who interrupts you

1. Go to **Controls**, then **Notifications**.
2. Tap **Open Notification access** and allow STOIC.
3. From then on, the **Today** card on that screen shows how many notifications each app sent.

## Hold the noisy ones

- Tap **Hold** next to an app. Its notifications are taken out of your notification shade and kept in STOIC.
- Pick when to get them under **Send my summary at** (for example 1 PM and 7 PM). You get one notification listing what was held.
- Tap a held notification in STOIC to open it, just as if you had tapped the original.

## During focus, bedtime and schedules

**Hold notifications from blocked apps** holds notifications from apps that are blocked at that moment, and sends a summary when the block ends.

## Never held

Calls, alarms, navigation, music and other ongoing notifications, and your phone and messages apps.

<Note>

Android lets STOIC hide a notification only after it arrives, so you may still hear the first sound. Bedtime's Do Not Disturb keeps nights fully quiet. Held notifications stay on your phone and are deleted when you open or clear them, or after three days.

</Note>

<IfItDidntWork>

- **No summary arrives**: allow notifications for STOIC in Android's app settings.
- **Android says "Restricted setting"**: see **Set up STOIC**.

</IfItDidntWork>
