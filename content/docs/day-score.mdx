## What it is

One number out of 100 for your day so far, made of four parts you control. It is a mirror, not a grade: there is no streak, and it stays on your phone.

## How it is worked out

- **Screen time (40 points)**: full points up to your daily goal, then fewer, down to zero at twice the goal.
- **Focus (25 points)**: your Pebble focus minutes towards your daily focus goal.
- **Choices (20 points)**: when a pause or limit screen appears, how often you choose **Go back**. A day with no prompts gets full points.
- **Bedtime (15 points)**: phone use during last night's Bedtime, down to zero at 30 minutes. Calls, messages and alarms don't count. If Bedtime is off, this part is left out and the others are scaled to 100.

Each part shows one sentence explaining its points.

## Set your goals

Go to **More**, then **My day score**, and pick your screen time goal (1 to 5 hours) and focus goal (25 to 100 minutes).

<Note>

A day gets a score in the week chart when you open STOIC that day.

</Note>
