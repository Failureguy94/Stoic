## When to use it

For the days you know you will be tempted: exams, deadlines, a late night. Set up your limits and schedules first; strict mode makes them firm.

## Start it

1. Go to **Controls**, then **Strict mode**.
2. Pick how long: 1 hour, 4 hours, until 7 AM, 1 day or 1 week.
3. Optional: **Also lock Settings** (the strongest option; it also locks Wi-Fi and other settings).
4. Tap **Start strict mode** and confirm.

## While it is on

- You can make things stricter (add apps, lower limits, add days), but not looser.
- **5 more minutes**, **Open once more** and schedule snoozes are hidden.
- A focus session that blocks apps can't be ended early.
- Uninstalling apps is blocked, and so is Settings if you chose that.
- Pauses still offer **Open anyway** and check-ins still offer **Keep going**: those are nudges, not limits.

You can extend strict mode while it is on, but not shorten it.

## Need out?

Tap **Unlock early** and wait 15 minutes. Often the urge passes by then. After the wait you can unlock, or choose to stay strict.

<Note>

Strict mode can't stop restarting the phone in safe mode, a computer with developer tools, or a factory reset.

</Note>
