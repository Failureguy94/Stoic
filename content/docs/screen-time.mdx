## See your screen time

Go to **More**, then **My screen time**. You see today's total, each app's time, and the last few days. It needs **Usage access**.

STOIC counts apps with an icon on your home screen. System screens and the home screen itself are left out.

## Keep your own record

Android keeps detailed history for only a few days, and on some phones it forgets an app's time when you uninstall it. Switch on **Record my daily screen time in STOIC** to keep your own copy:

- Every few hours, STOIC copies each day's totals into its own storage. Opening STOIC also records a check-in.
- Once a day is recorded, uninstalling another app can't erase it.
- Days when STOIC couldn't check in are marked **unverified**, not zero.

The record stays on your phone. Uninstalling STOIC or clearing its data deletes it.
