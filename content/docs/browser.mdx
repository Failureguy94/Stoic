## What it does

STOIC for your browser brings the same ideas to your laptop, in Chrome or Edge:

- **Pause before opening**: a 10-second pause before a distracting site, then **Open anyway**.
- **Daily limits**: "That's your time for today" once you have spent your minutes on a site. Time counts only while the browser is in front and your screen is unlocked, including while you watch a video.
- **Schedules**: hours when chosen sites stay closed, like study hours on weekdays.
- **Focus with Pebble**: start a 15, 25 or 45 minute session from the toolbar. Sites marked **Block during focus sessions** close until it ends.
- **A calmer YouTube**: hide the home feed, Shorts, recommendations, end-screen suggestions and comments. Any Short opens as a normal video.
- **Strict mode**: until the time you pick, rules can get stricter but not looser. You can also lock the browser's extensions page.

## Install it

The extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is:

1. Download the STOIC project and find the **extension** folder.
2. In Chrome, open **chrome://extensions** (in Edge, **edge://extensions**).
3. Switch on **Developer mode**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the **extension** folder.
5. Pin STOIC from the puzzle-piece menu so Pebble sits in your toolbar.

## Set it up

1. The settings page opens by itself after you install. Add the one or two sites that pull you in, or tap a suggestion like **+ youtube.com**.
2. Choose a pause, a daily limit, or both, and whether each site is blocked during focus.
3. Optional: add a schedule, and pick which parts of YouTube to hide.

<Note>

Everything stays in your browser. STOIC never sends anything anywhere, and it only counts time on the sites you added.

</Note>

<IfItDidntWork>

- **A site still opens**: check that the address matches. Adding **youtube.com** covers www.youtube.com and m.youtube.com, but not youtu.be.
- **Nothing happens in a private window**: extensions are off in private (Incognito or InPrivate) windows unless you allow them on the extension's **Details** page.
- **YouTube still shows something you hid**: YouTube changes its page often. Reload the page; if it is still there, let us know.

</IfItDidntWork>
